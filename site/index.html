Buying marine aquarium fish from the Philippines

Are you a licensed exporter in the Philippines?

Yes. Aquaphil Export Trading is on the Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR) list of registered live ornamental fish exporters, with registration No. OFNCR-LPS-01-09.

Which countries do you ship to?

We supply licensed importers and wholesalers outside the Philippines. Send your destination airport and we will confirm routing, flight days and landed-cost details.

Do you export saltwater fish and invertebrates?

Yes — marine ornamental fish such as clownfish, tangs, butterflyfish, wrasses and anthias, plus invertebrates like cleaner shrimp, hermit crabs, starfish and urchins.

Use the form below or message us on WhatsApp. We send the current stocklist with sizes and box prices.
